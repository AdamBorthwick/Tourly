Tourly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Tourly is a Chrome extension for building guided, scroll-synced video tours for webpages. This page explains what data Tourly collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

What Tourly collects

  • Tour content you create — the video you reference, scroll-point timing, subtitle text, page element selectors, and styling choices for each tour you build.
  • An anonymous install identifier — a random ID generated the first time you use Tourly, used only to keep your tours separate from anyone else's. It isn't linked to your name, email, or any other personal information.

Tourly does not collect your name, email address, payment details, or browsing history. The extension only activates on a page when you explicitly open it from the toolbar popup — it never runs in the background or observes pages you haven't opened it on.

How your data is used

Tour content and your anonymous identifier are sent to Supabase, our database provider, so your tours can be saved and restored across sessions. This data is never sold, and is not used for advertising or shared with data brokers.

Third-party services Tourly relies on

  • Supabase — stores tour data, associated with your anonymous install identifier.
  • vidzflow — the video hosting platform your tours reference; Tourly reads the public embed page for a video you provide to locate its playable file.
  • Cloudflare, jsDelivr, GitHub — serve the open-source tour-playback script used by tours you export. No personal data is sent to these services.
  • OpenAI (Whisper) — used only if you click "Auto-generate subtitles." The referenced video's audio is sent to OpenAI to produce a transcript, which becomes your tour's subtitles. This only happens when you explicitly request it.

Data deletion

Clearing the extension's storage (chrome://extensions → Tourly → "Clear data," or removing the extension) discards your local anonymous identity going forward. To request deletion of tours already stored in the cloud, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly.
